Privacy Policy

How AriseSell (NextProduct AI) collects, protects, processes, and respects merchant and customer data across WhatsApp Cloud API, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM, courier networks, and automated checkout.

Last Updated: September 4, 2026·Effective Date: Immediate·v2.4.0-BD

Table of Contents

Data Protection

We never sell customer conversations or use merchant proprietary stock for general AI model training.

01

1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to AriseSell (also operating under the technology identifier NextProduct AI, accessible via arisesell.com, alapai.app, and related portals). AriseSell is an enterprise SaaS platform engineered to provide autonomous conversational commerce, AI sales assistance, order management, and fulfillment automation for online merchants in Bangladesh and globally.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, process, and disclose information when you (the 'Merchant', 'Store Owner', or 'User') register for our services, connect your Meta/Facebook pages, WhatsApp Business numbers, courier accounts, and when end-consumers communicate with your automated storefront.

02

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide reliable commerce automation, conversational intelligence, and delivery dispatching:

A. Merchant Account Information

  • Full Name, business email, and phone number
  • Store / Brand name, logo, and physical location
  • Login credentials with secure bcrypt password hashing
  • Billing tier, subscription status, and invoice history

B. Messaging & Conversation Logs

  • Inbound customer messages (Text, Bangla/Banglish, Audio, Image screenshots)
  • Sender Platform User IDs (PSID, WABA Phone Number, IG User ID)
  • Conversation timestamps, message status, and AI responses

C. Customer Orders & Delivery Data

  • Customer delivery name, validated 11-digit phone number (01XXXXXXXXX)
  • Delivery address, District, and Thana / Upazila in Bangladesh
  • Itemized order variants, quantities, delivery charges, and COD totals

D. Connected Channel Credentials

  • Meta Page Access Tokens & WhatsApp Phone Number IDs
  • Steadfast API Key & Pathao Hermes Courier Credentials
  • bKash Merchant Keys / SSLCommerz Store IDs (Encrypted in DB)
03

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • Autonomous Conversational Sales: Enabling the AI Sales Assistant to answer customer product inquiries, parse Bangla/Banglish idioms, match product images, check stock availability, and collect checkout addresses.
  • Automated Courier Dispatch: Transmitting delivery address and COD amounts directly to Steadfast Courier or Pathao Courier APIs to generate official consignment tracking codes.
  • Merchant Operations & Live Inbox: Providing store owners with live monitoring of ongoing customer threads, instant manual takeover capabilities, order fulfillment metrics, and revenue analytics.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Validating HMAC-SHA256 signatures on inbound Meta webhooks, preventing replay attacks, and detecting fraudulent order submissions.
04

4. Meta Platform & WhatsApp Cloud API Compliance

AriseSell strictly adheres to the Meta Platform Terms, Developer Policies, and WhatsApp Business Messaging Policies.

When merchants connect their Facebook Pages, Instagram Accounts, or WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABA) to AriseSell:

  • We act solely as a Data Processor on behalf of the Merchant (the Data Controller).
  • Customer message contents and phone numbers received via Meta Graph / Cloud API are used strictly to reply to customer inquiries and facilitate the merchant's legitimate business transactions.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, broker, or monetize Meta user data or customer information.
  • Customer chat transcripts are isolated per-tenant and are never pooled into global foundation model training corpuses.
05

5. Third-Party Integrations & Sharing

To deliver full-stack e-commerce automation, we integrate with trusted third-party infrastructure providers:

Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp & Messenger Graph API)

Receiving webhook payloads and transmitting outbound automated chat bubbles via Meta's secure Cloud API edge.

Google Cloud & Gemini API (AI Reasoning Engine)

High-speed conversational inference, Bangla intent understanding, and product catalog grounding with enterprise data privacy guarantees.

Courier Partners (Steadfast Courier & Pathao Hermes)

Consignment creation, shipping label generation, and automated COD reconciliation across 64 districts of Bangladesh.

Payment Gateways (bKash & SSLCommerz)

Generating tokenized payment links and verifying transaction status. Sensitive payment credentials (card numbers, PINs) are processed directly on PCI-DSS certified gateway servers and are NEVER stored by AriseSell.

06

6. Security & Data Retention

We implement industry-grade technical and organizational security measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Database storage in PostgreSQL with tenant-isolated row policies and vector isolation.
  • Webhook verification with HMAC-SHA256 signatures to reject forged or spoofed requests.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and cryptographic token rotation for merchant consoles.

Conversation transcripts and order records are retained as long as the merchant's account remains active, or until the merchant requests their deletion via the console or legal request.

07

7. User Data Deletion Instructions (Meta Compliance)

In accordance with Meta Platform Policy and global data protection laws, any merchant or end-user has the right to request the complete deletion of their personal data and associated Meta conversation records.

How to Request Immediate Data Deletion:

  1. Send an email from your registered account to: hello@nextproduct.ai with the subject 'Data Deletion Request'.
  2. Or if you connected via Facebook Login / Meta App, visit your Facebook Profile > Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites > Find AriseSell > Click Remove, then click 'Send Request' for data deletion callback.
  3. Our automated data deletion service will purge your chat logs, tokens, and customer identity indices within 72 business hours and return a confirmation tracking code.
08

8. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to access, rectify, download, or restrict the processing of your personal information at any time directly through the AriseSell Merchant Console or by contacting our support team.

09

9. Cookies & Session Management

We use essential session cookies (np_access_token, np_refresh_token, np-lang) solely to authenticate merchant console logins, maintain security sessions, and preserve your preferred language (English or Bangla). We do not use third-party invasive ad tracking cookies.
